THE SERVICE

Business process assessment

A good fit for
For owners who feel their team is busy but cannot see where time is going or what to fix first.

Make it practical.
Make it yours.

A business process assessment looks at the everyday steps behind a service or internal task, from the first customer request through to completion. We document the current process with the people who do the work, identify where information is re-entered or jobs wait, and distinguish genuine constraints from habits that have simply become routine. The assessment focuses on the processes you choose, so the findings stay relevant to your team and goals.

You receive a clear summary of the current workflow, the main sources of friction and a prioritised set of improvement options. Some changes may be simple adjustments to ownership, forms or handovers; others may benefit from CRM configuration or automation. Recommendations are framed in terms of effort, dependencies and likely operational value, not as a list of tools to buy. This gives you a grounded starting point for deciding what to improve first and what should remain a human task.
