THE SERVICE

Websites for small businesses

A good fit for
For new businesses, outdated websites and service providers who need a clearer path from first visit to enquiry.

Make it practical.
Make it yours.

A small business website should answer the questions customers already have: what you offer, who you help, where you work and how to get started. We plan the page structure and write a clear content outline before shaping the design around your services and brand. The result is a responsive site that feels credible on a phone as well as a desktop, with readable service information, practical calls to action and a consistent visual style.

We can work with a new business starting from scratch or refresh an existing site that no longer represents the quality of the work. We also consider the foundations that make a site easier to maintain, including page titles, accessibility basics, loading performance and a simple editing approach. The goal is a useful business tool that supports enquiries and can grow as your services change.
