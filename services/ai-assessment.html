THE SERVICE

AI readiness assessment

A good fit for
For businesses considering AI tools but wanting to choose a safe, useful first step.

Make it practical.
Make it yours.

An AI readiness assessment helps a small business move from general interest in AI to a concrete, informed decision. We start with your goals and recurring tasks, then examine candidate use cases for practical value, data requirements, accuracy needs and the cost of an error. We also look at existing tools, how staff work today and whether a process is stable enough to support an AI feature.

The outcome is a short list of sensible next steps: opportunities worth testing, ideas that need more preparation and tasks that should stay with a person. We flag considerations such as privacy, customer disclosure, human review and access to business information. Where a pilot makes sense, we outline a small scope and success measures so you can learn before committing to a larger change. The assessment is designed to give you a balanced view, with recommendations tied to your business rather than AI for its own sake.
