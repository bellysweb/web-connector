THE SERVICE

CRM setup and improvement

A good fit for
For teams tracking leads in spreadsheets, missing follow-ups or working across inconsistent customer records.

Make it practical.
Make it yours.

CRM setup starts with understanding how enquiries, opportunities and customer relationships are handled today. We map your stages, agree what information is genuinely useful and configure records, fields, views and reminders to support that process. A clear structure helps staff see ownership and next actions without forcing them to fill in fields that have no purpose.

We can help with a new CRM or improve an existing one that has become difficult to use. The work may include organising pipelines, defining handover points, setting up straightforward follow-up reminders and reducing duplicate data entry where a reliable integration is available. We also consider permissions, data quality and team adoption. Your handover includes a simple explanation of the agreed process so people know what to update and when. The goal is consistent customer information and dependable follow-up, not a complicated system that creates more administration.
